What is going on right now

Straight summary for market participants

Core read

  • Kharg Island is the pressure point because it handles most of Iran's crude export flow. The latest escalation hit military assets there, but not the core oil infrastructure yet.
  • That means the market is pricing the threat of a bigger supply shock, not fully pricing a completed supply collapse.
  • If Kharg's oil infrastructure gets directly disabled, Iran's exports to China and broader Asia become materially harder and slower, even if some barrels still move through workarounds.
  • The bigger macro problem is not just Iran supply. It is the combined risk of disrupted shipping, higher insurance, delayed cargoes, and inflation pressure hitting global equities.
  • That is why broad equities can trade like a tariff tantrum replay: oil up, inflation fear up, yields and growth expectations in conflict, risk assets down.

What matters next

  • Whether the U.S. keeps the pressure at military targets only, or starts hitting the oil handling infrastructure itself.
  • Whether Hormuz traffic normalizes, even partially. If not, the oil risk premium stays sticky.
  • Whether China and Asian buyers keep receiving Iranian barrels via Kharg or reroutes like Jask and smaller workaround channels.
  • Whether equities stabilize after the first panic leg down, or start another round of de-risking like a policy shock spiral.
